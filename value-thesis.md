## Value thesis
Northfield Mutual wants routine policyholder correspondence sent sooner after an approved claim decision, while claims handlers continue to verify every detail and approve every letter. Today, letters take a median of 2.4 business days to send after approval. The pilot has reached **Work Change**: 26 of 40 handlers used the drafting workflow more than once, sampled median drafting time fell from 18 to 11 minutes, and reviewed drafts needed fewer wording revisions. The evidence supports one value claim now: the workflow reduces handler drafting time and wording rework. It doesn't yet show that letters reach policyholders sooner than the 2.4-day baseline.

## Purpose and outcome
- **Leader:** I'm the AI Transformation Program Manager, and I lead this initiative (your correction, 2026-10-04). The earlier claims operations director role, and the reporting lines stated with it, are withdrawn.
- **Purpose:** lead my team in using AI responsibly to cut repetitive drafting and rework on routine policyholder correspondence at Northfield Mutual (fictional).
- **Outcome:** routine updates reach waiting policyholders more promptly, letters are correct, and policyholders are less frustrated.
- **Fixed control (from the case):** AI may prepare a first draft. A claims handler checks every detail, makes any needed changes and approves every letter before it is sent.
- **Measures (answered 2026-10-03):** correctness = fewer policyholder complaints about operators' handling of their policy; frustration = manual review of policyholder feedback. **Open:** the starting point for each measure (Q9), and which updates count as routine (Q5).

## How I will lead
- **Firsthand use:** I use AI to draft initial plans, including this Charter. I also use it to record deliverables, time and cost budgets, resource limits, problem complexity and a decision log.
- **Visible learning (Proposed):** share my AI-drafted plans and decision log with my team, showing what I kept, changed or rejected after reviewing the AI output. Share what each retrospective teaches.
- **Responsible experiments:** run bounded tests only (A4), within my assigned resources, with the handler-approval control above applied to every letter.
- **Data handling (Proposed):** no real policyholder, claim or policy data goes into AI tools until it has been checked against the organisation's data-handling rules.
- **Constructive response to failed tests:** record the failure mode and bring together everyone accountable for that part of the work. Hold a blameless retrospective, agree fixes so it doesn't happen again, and close off loose ends.

## Overall
The structure covers all six sections and all four evidence levels. Before it goes to a receiving team, I'd treat gaps **1, 2, 3, 5 and 6** as blocking, because the team can't start without a tool, access, a start rule, an owner and a support route. The rest can be fixed during the introduction. All are suggestions until you decide.

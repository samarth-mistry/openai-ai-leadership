## Summary
- **Priority audience:** claims handlers (40 in the pilot).
- **Adoption stage:** Experimenting, **uncertain**. 26 of 40 used the workflow more than once. The other 14 are Not Started. *(Support recommendation v2, Draft)*
- **Intended behaviour:** AI prepares a first draft of a routine letter. The handler verifies every detail, edits and approves every letter before it is sent, and reports results weekly. *(Reviewed Operating Model)*
- **Primary barrier:** Access. The 14 non-users were denied access and had configuration problems (your report, 2026-10-05). The fix is **not confirmed**.
- **Workflow owner:** Claims Correspondence Operations Manager. *(Reviewed)*
- **Adoption coordinator:** you, as AI Transformation Program Manager and AI adoption lead. No separate coordinator is needed unless you say otherwise. *(Reviewed; Assumption that none is needed)*
- **Agreed boundary:** continue within the current pilot scope. A successful pilot doesn't authorise routine use or any expansion. Replies to policyholder concerns, end-of-day updates and conflict or legal cases stay out of the pilot for now (your decision, 2026-10-05; Q5 working assumption). *(Reviewed value thesis and Operating Model)*
- **Escalation route:** access or configuration problems go to you, then the Enterprise Applications Administrator. Data-handling problems go to you, then the Automation Solutions Architect, with the Workflow owner reviewing and changing it. Policy, privacy or risk concerns go to the Responsible AI and Privacy Working Group. A draft a handler can't verify goes to the Workflow owner, and the letter isn't sent until a handler approves it. Capacity or barriers you can't clear go to the Chief Claims Officer. A failed test leads to a blameless retrospective. *(Reviewed Operating Model)*

**Quality, human checks and safeguards (run across all levels)**
- **Human check (Reviewed):** a handler verifies every detail and approves every letter before it is sent. **Proposal:** the Workflow owner confirms each week from the handlers' reports that no letter went out without handler approval.
- **Quality (Proposal):** wording revisions per reviewed draft, plus errors or wrong details that handlers catch in drafts. Whether the errors are recorded isn't confirmed.
- **Safeguards:** conflict and legal cases stay out of scope. No real policyholder or claim data in practice or new use until Q18 is answered (Proposed Charter rule). Issues found go in the weekly report.
- **Usage alone isn't business impact.** Use and drafting time show Work Change at most. Business Impact needs the approval-to-send comparison.

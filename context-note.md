## Confirmed
Supplied by the case or by the user.

- **Organisation:** Northfield Mutual, a fictional insurer with about 2,000 employees.
- **Lead:** the AI Transformation Program Manager (the user), running a bounded initiative on routine policyholder correspondence. *(Corrected by the user 2026-10-04; the case text names the claims operations director.)*
- **Priority:** reduce repetitive drafting and rework for claims handlers, so routine updates reach waiting policyholders more promptly.
- **People affected:** claims handlers, policyholders, team leaders, managers and compliance reviewers.
- **Current work:** handlers check the triggers and validate claims against policies. They draft and redraft letters, and they write back and forth with the policyholder when there are conflicts. They recreate similar wording and check details across several systems.
- **Useful result:** correct letters, faster responses and less frustration for policyholders.
- **Human role set by the case:** a claims handler verifies every detail, makes any needed changes and approves each letter before it is sent.

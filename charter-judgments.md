# Charter judgments

Status: **Reviewed** (approved by the user, 2026-10-03). These are inputs for the Charter, not the Charter itself.

## The user's role
- A mid-level leader. Compliance reviewers, other managers and insurance operators report to them.

## 1. Modelling firsthand use and visible review
- The user will use the AI model to draft initial plans, including the Charter itself.
- They'll use it to record deliverables, time budgets, cost budgets, resource limits, problem complexity, and a decision log.

## 2. Responding when a bounded test fails
- Record the failure mode.
- Bring together everyone accountable for that part of the work.
- Hold a blameless retrospective.
- Agree mitigations so the failure doesn't recur, and close off loose ends.

## 3. Where the user's authority ends
- **The user can decide:** how to use and reshuffle the resources already assigned to them, according to priority.
- **Must go to sponsors:** any additional resources, or any time budget beyond the user's authority.
- **Sponsors must reinforce and protect:** the deliverables agreed at the start.
- **Sponsors must unblock:** roadblocks the team raises and presents to them.
- **Sponsors must review:** the statistics, periodically.

# Case context: Northfield Mutual (fictional)

Source: supplied by the user, 2026-09-23. This is the approved case context for the project. Treat any other detail as **Proposed** or **Unresolved** unless the course supplies it or the user approves it.

- Northfield Mutual is a fictional insurer with about 2,000 employees.
- Its claims operations director is leading a bounded initiative to improve routine policyholder correspondence. *(Original case text. The user corrected their role on 2026-10-04: they play the AI Transformation Program Manager, who leads the initiative in this project.)*
- Claims handlers currently recreate similar wording and check details across several systems.
- **Opportunity:** reduce repetitive drafting and rework for claims handlers, so routine updates reach waiting policyholders more promptly.
- **AI and human roles (as stated):** AI can help prepare a first draft. A claims handler verifies every detail, makes any needed changes, and approves the letter before it is sent.

| Step | What the handler does | Current problem |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Gather information | Collects the approved decision, policy and customer details, case notes and required wording from authorised systems | Repeated switching and copying between systems |

| 3. Review the draft | Checks facts, tone and required statements | Details can be difficult to trace to their sources |
